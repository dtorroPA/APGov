# Gerrymander Tour

An interactive gerrymandering simulator for AP Government. A state of 270 voters, made up of two music fanbases, is split into 10 equal-population districts. Students pick the fanbases and the map-maker's goal, then watch the district lines redraw themselves.

**To use it, open `index.html` in any web browser.** No install is needed. It also works on GitHub Pages.

## Map-maker's goals
- **Compact & neutral**: tidy shapes that ignore who lives where
- **Favor A / Favor B**: packing and cracking to win the most seats
- **Competitive**: every district as close to 50/50 as possible
- **Proportional**: each side's share of seats matches its share of votes

The scoreboard shows the popular vote, seats won, the fair (proportional) share, toss-up districts, the efficiency gap, and a compactness score. The page also covers key terms, Baker v. Carr, Shaw v. Reno, Rucho v. Common Cause, and discussion questions.
